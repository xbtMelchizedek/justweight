justweight

Quote one inference job across FLOP, Pearl, Venice, Bittensor (Chutes), each OpenRouter provider and your own machine. Every price links to where it came from. Prices with no source stay blank; nothing is guessed.

Standard unit by tier

1 IU = 750k input + 250k output tokens of the tier's reference model, at the cheapest offer per venue. List price first; OpenRouter's published effective price (after cache hits) below it. FLOP fills from settled receipts once testnet lists models.
